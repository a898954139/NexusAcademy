Nexus Media Automation

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the internal Nexus publishing client accesses, uses, stores and deletes data obtained through Google OAuth and YouTube API Services.

Effective: 27 July 2026 Last updated: 27 July 2026

1. Operator and scope

Who operates this service

Nexus Media Automation is an internal publishing tool operated by Chan Wei Feng for Nexus. Questions or verified privacy requests may be sent to mynexustrading@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the client's use of Google OAuth and YouTube API Services. The client is restricted to authorised Nexus operators and is not offered as a public application.

2. Data collected

Information the client processes

  • Google OAuth credentials required to maintain an authorised connection.
  • The authenticated YouTube channel ID, channel name and ownership-related metadata returned by channels.list.
  • Video identifiers, titles, descriptions, processing state and visibility metadata returned by videos.list.
  • Original video files, thumbnails and publishing metadata supplied by authorised Nexus operators.
  • Approval, delivery, error and API readback records needed to operate and audit the workflow.

The client does not collect YouTube viewers' account data, search histories, subscriptions, comments, contacts or advertising profiles.

3. Use of information

Why the data is used

  • To verify that the authorised account controls the intended Nexus YouTube channel.
  • To upload owner-approved original educational videos and metadata.
  • To verify upload delivery, processing state and privacy status.
  • To diagnose failures, maintain security and preserve an accountable approval record.

Google-derived data is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or the sale of personal information.

4. Sharing and disclosure

No sale of Google user data

Nexus does not sell, rent or trade Google user data. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers only when necessary to operate or secure the client, or disclosed when required by applicable law. Access is limited to the operator and authorised Nexus personnel with a legitimate operational need.

5. Storage and security

How information is protected

OAuth credentials are kept in local secret storage and are excluded from source control, public evidence and approval messages. The workflow uses access controls, approval gates, channel-identity checks and post-upload readback checks. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, but reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are maintained.

6. Retention

How long information is kept

  • OAuth credentials are retained until the operator revokes or disconnects the integration.
  • Operational and API readback logs are retained for up to 90 days unless a longer period is required for security, legal compliance or the investigation of a publishing incident.
  • Original media, publishing metadata and approval records may be retained as Nexus business records.

7. Controls, revocation and deletion

How to remove access or request deletion

  1. Revoke the client's Google access through Google Account connections.
  2. Email mynexustrading@gmail.com with a verified deletion request.

Upon revocation, the client will no longer be able to use the revoked authorisation. OAuth credentials associated with the disconnected account will be deleted promptly. Google-derived operational data covered by a verified deletion request will normally be deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law, security obligations or the preservation of legitimate business records.

8. YouTube and Google policies

Third-party terms

The client uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and YouTube API Services Developer Policies. Google's processing of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the integration, legal requirements or YouTube API policies change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.
